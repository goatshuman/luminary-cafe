Our Story

Born from a love of
craft & community

Luminary began as a dream shared over a late-night espresso — a place where slow mornings are celebrated, where the beans are sourced with care, and where every guest feels at home from the first hello.

We partner directly with small-batch farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, building relationships that ensure fair wages and exceptional quality in every cup we serve.
